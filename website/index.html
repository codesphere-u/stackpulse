Frequently Asked Questions

Telemetry & SRE details

How is exclusive span time computed when children overlap asynchronously?

TraceAnalyzer sorts and merges overlapping child intervals [start, end] before subtracting their union length from the parent span duration.

How are alert webhooks protected against SSRF?

/api/v1/alerts/register-webhook blocks loopback, private RFC1918, IPv6-mapped IPv4, link-local metadata (`169.254.169.254`), and `.internal` targets by default.
